Brand History

Since 1925, the Loacker family has been crafting delicious wafer and chocolate specialties in the heart of the Italian Alps. What began as a small bakery in Bolzano has grown into a global brand known for quality and taste.

For generations, we've maintained our commitment to natural ingredients, authentic recipes, and sustainable practices - creating pure moments of enjoyment for millions of people worldwide

Why Loacker

Our Italian Hazelnut Orchards

  • We only use 100% Italian hazelnuts.
  • Grown partially in our own orchards.

Our Cocoa Farming Program

With our project we support cooperatives and farmers in Ecuador and the Ivory Coast.

Our NON-GMO Alpine Milk

Our milk is 100% from small mountain farms in the Alps and is NON GMO.

Our Bourbon Vanilla from Madagascar

Working with local small farmers makes our vanilla sustainable, transparent and fair.

Our Products

Quadratini

These bite-sized, crunchy cubes with five light, crispy wafers and four layers of the smoothest cream filling will tempt you to enjoy more than one of Loacker’s Quadratini wafer treats!

Gardena

This uniquely creamy wafer creation forms the perfect blend of the smoothest cream filling, light and crispy wafers and fine chocolate—creating an extremely harmonious pleasure.

Minis

The Classic Minis, in their practical small size, are the perfect companion throughout the day. They win you over with their three delicious layers of tenderly melting cream filling and four light, crispy wafers!

Chocolate Bar

You will experience unique moments of pleasure with Loacker’s Chocolate Bars. Creamy, crisp, and full of delicious chocolate flavor—made of premium quality milk and fine ingredients.

Classic

The secret to Loacker’s Classic Wafers’ success is based on natural ingredients and special recipes. Perfect for lunches and light snacks. 45g for self, 175g to share!

Patisserie

Love at first bite! Gran Pasticceria Patisserie tantalizes the senses with light, crispy wafers, fine cream, and delicious chocolate glazing.

Tortina

A perfect creation full of finesse: Tortina has a core of light, crispy wafers and the finest hazelnut cream hidden underneath a delicious layer of chocolate.

Chocolat

Loacker’s Chocolat tantalizes the senses with light, crispy wafers and delicious chocolate glazing. Underneath: tenderly melting cream filling.
